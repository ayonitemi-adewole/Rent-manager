Getting started

Who is RentPadi for?

Nigerian landlords who collect rent themselves, or with a caretaker, from roughly 4 to 50 units: flats, mini-flats, rooms or shops. If you've ever chased rent by phone, kept records in a notebook or argued about whether someone paid, it's for you.

How long does setup take?

Most landlords add their properties, units and tenants in about 10 minutes on a phone. A short checklist and on-screen tour guide you through it. Add your bank account last, so tenants can pay online.

Do I need to download an app?

No. RentPadi works in your phone's browser. You can add it to your home screen so it opens like an app. Your tenants never need to install anything.

How do I log in?

With your phone number. We send a one-time code by WhatsApp or SMS. There's no password to remember.

Some of my tenants already paid for this year. Will they show as owing?

No. When you add a tenant, enter the date their rent is paid up to. RentPadi starts their reminders from the next due date.

Can you help me set it up?

Yes. We're happy to set it up with you, in person if you're in Ibadan or on a call anywhere else. Get in touch.

Payments and money

Does RentPadi hold my money?

No, never. Online payments go through a licensed payment company and settle straight into your own bank account. RentPadi has no balance of yours and can't move your money. More on security.

How do tenants pay?

Through their private link, by card, bank transfer or USSD. They can also message RentPadi on WhatsApp to get the link. If they pay you in cash or by transfer to your account, record it in the app.

When does the money reach my account?

It follows the payment company's normal settlement times, usually the next working day. RentPadi marks the rent as paid as soon as the payment is confirmed, often within seconds.

Can tenants pay part of the rent?

Yes. Each part payment gets a receipt showing the balance left, and reminders only ask for what's still owed. You can never record more than a tenant owes.

What if I record a cash payment by mistake?

Void it with a short reason. The entry stays in your history marked VOID, so your records stay honest, and the balance goes back to what it was.

Can I change the bank account rent goes to?

Yes, in Settings → Payout account. We confirm the account name with you before saving. Future payments go to the new account.

Reminders

When do reminders go out?
  • Yearly rent: 60, 30, 14, 7 and 1 day before it's due.
  • Six-monthly rent: 30, 14, 7 and 1 day before.
  • Monthly rent: 14, 7 and 1 day before.
  • If it's late: 1, 7 and 14 days after the due date.

Padi Free sends 2 SMS reminders per rent bill. Paid plans send the full schedule on WhatsApp, with SMS as a backup.

What do reminders say?

Something like: “Hi Tola, your rent of N350,000 for Flat 2 is due 1 Dec. Pay securely: [link] – Mr Adeyemi”. Short, polite and signed with your name.

Can I stop reminders for one tenant?

Yes. Pause reminders on the tenant's page, for example if you've agreed a later date. You can also send a reminder right away on WhatsApp with one tap.

Will tenants think it's a scam?

It helps to tell your tenants you're starting to use RentPadi before the first reminder. Every message includes your name, and their rent page shows your bank and account ending. Our tenant safety guide explains what to check.

Tenants

Do my tenants need a smartphone?

No. Reminders also go by SMS to any phone, the link works in any browser, and USSD works on any phone. Cash payments can be recorded too.

Do tenants pay a fee?

No. Tenants pay exactly their rent, on every plan.

Do tenants need to create an account?

No. Their private link opens their rent page directly. On WhatsApp, they're recognised by the number you registered for them.

Can tenants see other tenants' details?

No. A tenant only ever sees their own unit, balance and receipts.

Plans and billing

How much does RentPadi cost?
  • Padi Free: ₦0 for up to 5 units, plus 1% of rent paid online, max ₦2,000 per rent bill.
  • Padi: ₦24,000 a year (or ₦2,400 a month) for up to 10 units.
  • Padi Plus: ₦50,000 a year (or ₦5,000 a month) for up to 25 units.
  • Padi Pro: ₦100,000 a year (or ₦10,000 a month) for up to 50 units, then ₦200 per extra unit a month.

Paid plans take no percentage of your rent. See full pricing.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every new account gets 30 days of Padi Plus free. After that, choose a plan, or do nothing and you'll move to Padi Free. We'll tell you before anything changes.

What happens if I don't renew?

You get a 7-day grace period with reminders, then move to Padi Free. You keep your records, your tenants can still pay, and you can export everything. Features from your paid plan pause; your settings are remembered.

What if I go over my plan's unit limit?

You get 14 days to upgrade. We never stop you recording or receiving rent. On Padi Pro, extra units simply cost ₦200 each a month.

Do you charge VAT?

No. RentPadi is a small business under the VAT threshold, so the prices shown are the full prices.

Data and safety

Who can see my records?

Only you. Each landlord's data is separated at the database level. Tenants see only their own rent page.

Can I export my data?

Yes, on every plan. Download all payments, or one tenant's statement, as a CSV file that opens in Excel or Google Sheets.

How do you use my tenants' phone numbers?

Only to send rent reminders and receipts on your behalf, and to recognise them on WhatsApp. We never sell them or send marketing. We follow Nigeria's Data Protection Act 2023. See our privacy notice.

Yes. Email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} from your account's details and we'll remove it. Export your records first if you want to keep them.
