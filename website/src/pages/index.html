Questions

Landlords usually ask

Does RentPadi hold my money?

No. Online payments settle straight from the payment company to your own bank account. RentPadi never holds a balance for you, and we can't move your money.

My tenants are not on smartphones. Will this work?

Yes. Reminders go by SMS to any phone, and the payment link works in any browser. Tenants can also pay by USSD or bank transfer. If they pay you in cash, record it and they still get a receipt.

Do my tenants pay any extra fee?

No. Tenants pay exactly their rent on every plan. The payment company's fee comes out of the amount you receive, at cost, and we show it to you on every payment.

What if a tenant pays part of the rent?

That's fine. RentPadi records the part payment, gives a receipt, shows the balance left and only reminds the tenant about what's still owed.

Can I stop using it and take my records?

Yes, any time. You can export your full payment history to a spreadsheet on every plan, including Padi Free.
