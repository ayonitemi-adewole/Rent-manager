How fees work

Tenants pay exactly the rent. Always.

No surprise charges at checkout. Here's where every naira of a ₦100,000 rent goes on Padi Free:

  • The payment company's fee comes out of what you receive, at cost. We add nothing on top, and paying by bank transfer is the cheapest option for everyone.
  • On Padi Free, RentPadi keeps 1%, capped at ₦2,000 per rent bill, however the tenant pays it. A ₦1M rent paid in four parts costs ₦2,000 in total, not ₦8,000.
  • On paid plans, RentPadi keeps nothing from your rent.
  • Refunds include our fee. If a rent payment is refunded, so is the RentPadi fee on it.

Pricing questions

Good to know

Why is yearly billing the default?

Most Nigerian rent is paid yearly, so most landlords already think in years. Paying yearly also gives you 2 months free. Monthly billing is there if you prefer it.

When does a paid plan save me money over Padi Free?

If your tenants pay monthly, Padi usually saves you money from your 3rd tenant: 3 × ₦1,000 a month on Free is more than ₦2,400 a month on Padi. If your tenants pay yearly, Padi Free costs at most ₦10,000 a year at its 5-unit limit. Landlords on yearly rent move to Padi for WhatsApp reminders, receipts with their own name and room to grow. Try the calculator with your own numbers.

Does the 1% apply to cash payments I record?

No. The Padi Free fee only applies to rent paid online through RentPadi. Cash or direct transfers you record yourself carry no RentPadi fee.

What is the payment company's fee?

The licensed payment company that moves the money charges a small fee on each online payment. It comes out of the amount you receive, at cost, and we show it on every payment. Bank transfer is the cheapest way to pay (around ₦322.50 on any amount). Card costs more (up to about ₦2,150).

Is there a discount for early landlords?

Yes. Our first 30 founding landlords get Padi Plus free for 12 months, then half price for the next 12, in return for feedback. Apply to be a founding landlord.

I'm an agent or property manager. Is there a plan for me?

Padi Pro covers up to 50 units with up to 5 caretaker accounts, then ₦200 per extra unit a month. If you manage property for several landlords, tell us how you work. We're planning a plan for agents.
