Properties, units and tenants

Your whole portfolio, entered in minutes

Flats, mini-flats, rooms in a face-me-I-face-you, shops: add them the way you think about them. A guided first setup walks you from your first property to your first tenant.

Properties and units

Name each building, then add its units with a label, type and rent. "Save and add another" makes a 10-room house quick to enter.

Any rent period

Yearly, every six months or monthly. A tenancy that starts on the 31st stays on the 31st, or the last day of shorter months, and never drifts.

"Paid up to" for existing tenants

Tell RentPadi when a tenant's current rent runs out. They won't show as overdue for rent they paid before you joined.

Agreed amount per tenant

The unit has a standard rent, but each tenant can have their own agreed amount. Change it later and future bills follow.

Move-outs and unit history

Mark a tenant as moved out and the unit is free for the next. Every unit keeps a list of everyone who has lived there.

A guided first setup

A short checklist and an on-screen tour point at each button: property, unit, tenant, bank account. No manual needed.

Automatic reminders

Polite, on time, and never forgotten

Every morning RentPadi checks who needs a reminder and sends it. Each reminder goes out once, even if something retries. Paid bills are skipped, and part-paid bills ask only for the balance.

Online payments

A payment link for every tenant, settled to your bank

Each tenant gets a private, unguessable link. It opens a simple page, with no app and no login, showing your name, what they owe and a Pay button. They pay by card, bank transfer or USSD.

  • Settles to your own bank account. We confirm the account name with you when you add it, so a typo never sends rent to a stranger.
  • Always the right amount. The checkout is created when the tenant taps Pay, using the balance at that moment.
  • Oldest bill first. Tenants pay one period at a time, so nobody skips a year.
  • Verified, then recorded. We check each payment with the payment company before marking it paid. A repeated notice never counts twice.
  • Reset a link if it's shared too widely. The old one stops working at once.

Cash and direct transfers

Cash payments in the same ledger, in three taps

Some tenants will always hand you cash or send a transfer straight to your account. Record it and RentPadi treats it like any other payment: the balance updates, reminders stop and a receipt is ready to share.

Amount, method, date

Cash or transfer, the date it was paid, and an optional note or bank reference. That's it.

Part payments allowed

Record ₦150,000 of ₦350,000 and the balance shows ₦200,000. You can never record more than is owed.

Mistakes are voided, not deleted

Void a wrong entry with a reason. The record stays, marked VOID, so your history is always honest.
