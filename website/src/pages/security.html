Security and trust

Built so you never have to wonder where the money is

Trust is the whole product. Here, in plain words, is how RentPadi handles your rent, your records and your tenants' details.

Rule number one

RentPadi never holds your money

We are software, not a bank or a wallet. When a tenant pays online, a licensed payment company moves the money and settles it into your own bank account. There's no RentPadi balance to withdraw from, and nothing for us to hold back.

Tenant pays

Card, transfer or USSD on a secure checkout

Payment company

Licensed, card details never touch RentPadi

Your bank

Settled to the account you confirmed

We'll never offer wallets, "rent savings" or escrow that would mean holding your money. If that ever changes, it will only be through a licensed bank partner, and we'll tell you first.

Payments

Every payment is checked before it counts

Your account name, confirmed

When you add your bank account, we look up the account name and show it to you before saving. A wrong digit can't quietly send rent to a stranger.

Verified twice

When the payment company tells us a payment succeeded, we check the message is genuine, then ask them directly to confirm the amount, currency and status.

Never counted twice

Each payment has a unique reference. If the same notice arrives again, nothing changes. Your balances stay correct.

Keys stay on our servers

The secret keys that talk to payment and messaging companies live only on our servers, never in the app on your phone.

Private, resettable tenant links

Each tenant link has a long random code that can't be guessed. If one gets passed around, reset it and the old link stops working at once.

Tenants see only their own rent

A tenant's page shows their unit, what they owe and their receipts. Nothing about your other tenants, ever.

Records

A ledger you can trust in a dispute

Your rent records are only useful if nobody can quietly rewrite them, not even by accident.

  • Money is stored in kobo, as whole numbers, so there are no rounding errors.
  • Mistakes are voided with a reason, never deleted. The original entry stays, marked VOID.
  • Receipt numbers never skip, so a missing receipt is easy to spot.
  • Each landlord's data is walled off at the database level. Even a bug in the app can't show you someone else's tenants.

Your data

Your records belong to you

No passwords to steal

You log in with a one-time code sent to your phone by WhatsApp or SMS. There's no password to reuse, forget or leak.

Nigeria's Data Protection Act 2023

We collect only what's needed to remind, collect and receipt rent. We never sell your data or your tenants' data. Read our privacy notice.

Export any time
