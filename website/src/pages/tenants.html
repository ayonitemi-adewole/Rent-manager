Paying is easy

  1. You get a reminder

    An SMS or WhatsApp message with your landlord's name, the amount, the due date and your private link.

  2. Open your link

    It shows your unit, what you owe and who you're paying. No login needed.

  3. Pay your way

    Tap Pay and choose card, bank transfer or USSD. You can also pay part now and the rest later.

  4. Get your receipt

    A numbered receipt is ready straight away. Keep it, print it, or ask for it again on WhatsApp.

Stay safe

Is this message really from RentPadi?

Scammers copy real messages. Here's how to tell the difference before you pay anything.

A real RentPadi message

  • Uses your name, your unit and your landlord's name.
  • Links to your rent page on RentPadi's own web address.
  • Your rent page shows your landlord's bank and the last 4 digits of their account.
  • Asks for the same amount you agreed with your landlord.

RentPadi will never

  • Ask for your card PIN, OTP or bank password in a message or call.
  • Ask you to transfer rent to a personal account named in a chat.
  • Add a "processing fee" on top of your rent.
  • Threaten you or rush you to pay within minutes.

Not sure? Call your landlord on the number you already have before paying. If something looks wrong, email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} and we'll check it for you.

Tenant questions

Questions tenants ask

Do I pay any extra fee to RentPadi?

No. You pay exactly your rent. Any fees are paid by your landlord. Your own bank may charge the government's ₦50 stamp duty on transfers of ₦10,000 or more. That's the law, not a RentPadi fee.

Who receives my money?

Your landlord, directly. The payment goes from you, through a licensed payment company, straight to your landlord's bank account. Your rent page shows which bank and the last four digits of the account.

I paid cash. Will I still get reminders?

Once your landlord records your cash payment, reminders stop and a receipt is ready for you. If you keep getting reminders after paying, let your landlord know.

Can I pay part of my rent now?

Yes, if your landlord accepts it. Every part payment gets a receipt showing what's left, and reminders only ask for the balance.

I lost my link. What do I do?

Ask your landlord to send it again, or message RentPadi on WhatsApp from the number your landlord registered. If your link has been shared with someone it shouldn't have, your landlord can reset it, and the old one stops working.

What do you do with my information?

We only keep what's needed to remind you and receipt your rent: your name, phone number, unit and payments. We never sell it, and we follow Nigeria's Data Protection Act 2023. See our privacy notice.
