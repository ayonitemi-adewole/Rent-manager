About us

Every landlord deserves a padi for rent

“Padi” means a friend who has your back. RentPadi is that friend for rent: it remembers every due date, it keeps every receipt, and it does the reminding so you don't have to.

Our story

We started by listening

Before writing any code, we sat down with landlords in Ibadan who rent out flats, rooms and shops. We asked how they collected rent the last time it was due.

The answers were the same everywhere. Calls and visits every month. Notebooks with crossed-out dates. Arguments about whether a tenant had paid. Some landlords paid caretakers a large share of the rent just to chase it, and some never saw all of what was collected.

The tools that existed were built for big property companies, or for other countries. None of them suited a retired teacher with eight rooms and a WhatsApp account.

So we built RentPadi to do one job well: remind tenants, collect rent straight into the landlord's own account, and keep a record nobody can argue with.

What we believe

The rules we build by

Money is never ours

Rent goes straight from tenant to landlord. We never hold it, pool it or invest it. Our business is software, not your money.

The ledger is the product

Every naira is recorded exactly, nothing is silently overwritten, and every receipt is numbered. Records have to hold up when there's a disagreement.

Simple enough for anyone

Many landlords are over 50. If something needs a manual, we've built it wrong. Plain words, big buttons, nothing to install.

Built for real phones

We test on mid-range Android phones over slow connections, because that's what many landlords and tenants use.

Fair to both sides

Tenants pay exactly their rent and always get a receipt. Landlords keep their records, even if they stop paying us.

Shipped with landlords

We build in small steps, put each one in front of real landlords, and change course when they tell us something isn't working.
